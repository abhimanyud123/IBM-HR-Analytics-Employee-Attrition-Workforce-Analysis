# Business Questions

## Overview

The objective of this analysis is to understand employee attrition, workforce composition, compensation, employee satisfaction, and workforce trends using the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

The following business questions guide the SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard development.

---

## 1. Executive KPIs

The project will answer the following high-level business questions:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- How many employees are included in the dataset?
- What is the average employee age?
- What is the average monthly income?

---

## 2. Department Analysis

The following questions evaluate workforce distribution and attrition across departments:

- Which departments have the highest employee attrition?
- Which departments have the highest average monthly income?
- How are employees distributed across departments?

---

## 3. Job Role Analysis

These questions analyze employee turnover and compensation by job role:

- Which job roles have the highest employee attrition?
- Which job roles receive the highest average monthly income?
- How are employees distributed across different job roles?

---

## 4. Employee Demographics Analysis

These questions examine how employee characteristics relate to attrition:

- Which age groups experience the highest attrition?
- Does marital status influence employee attrition?
- Is employee attrition different across genders?

---

## 5. Compensation Analysis

The following questions evaluate the relationship between employee compensation and attrition:

- Does monthly income differ between employees who leave and those who remain?
- Are lower-paid employees more likely to leave the organization?

---

## 6. Employee Satisfaction Analysis

These questions measure the impact of employee satisfaction on retention:

- How does job satisfaction relate to employee attrition?
- Does work-life balance influence employee attrition?
- Does environment satisfaction affect employee attrition?

---

## 7. Workforce Experience Analysis

These questions evaluate employee tenure and career progression:

- Does employee tenure affect attrition?
- Are newer employees more likely to leave the organization?

---

## 8. Work Pattern Analysis

These questions analyze workplace conditions associated with employee attrition:

- Does overtime influence employee attrition?
- Does business travel affect employee attrition?

---

## Expected Business Outcomes

The analysis is expected to provide insights that can help organizations:

- Identify the primary factors associated with employee attrition.
- Improve employee retention strategies.
- Support workforce planning and talent management.
- Enhance employee satisfaction and work-life balance initiatives.
- Review compensation and overtime policies.
- Identify high-risk departments and job roles.
- Enable data-driven HR decision-making through interactive dashboards.
