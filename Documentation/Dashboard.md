# Dashboard Documentation

## Dashboard Objective

The Power BI dashboard was developed to present key business insights from the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Analysis project. It provides an interactive summary of workforce demographics, employee attrition, and workplace factors associated with employee turnover using data analyzed through PostgreSQL and visualized in Power BI.

The dashboard is designed for HR professionals, managers, and business leaders, enabling them to monitor employee attrition trends and identify factors that may influence employee retention.

---

# Dashboard Structure

The dashboard consists of two pages:

1. Executive Overview
2. Employee Insights

Each page focuses on a different level of workforce analysis while maintaining a consistent design and reporting structure.

---

# Page 1: Executive Overview

The Executive Overview provides a high-level summary of the workforce and employee attrition through key performance indicators and visualizations.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income

### Visualizations

#### Employee Attrition by Department

Compares employee attrition across departments to identify areas with the highest turnover.

#### Employee Attrition by Job Role

Displays attrition across job roles to highlight positions with the greatest employee turnover.

#### Employee Attrition by Age Group

Shows attrition trends across different employee age groups.

#### Employee Distribution by Gender

Illustrates the workforce distribution by gender.

#### Employee Distribution by Marital Status

Displays the distribution of employees by marital status.

---

# Page 2: Employee Insights

The Employee Insights page provides a deeper analysis of workplace factors associated with employee attrition.

### Visualizations

#### Employee Attrition by Overtime

Compares attrition between employees who worked overtime and those who did not.

#### Employee Attrition by Business Travel

Shows employee attrition across different business travel categories.

#### Average Monthly Income by Attrition

Compares average monthly income between employees who stayed and employees who left.

#### Employee Attrition by Years at Company

Illustrates how employee attrition changes with company tenure.

#### Employee Attrition by Job Satisfaction

Displays employee attrition across different job satisfaction levels.

#### Employee Attrition by Work-Life Balance

Shows the relationship between work-life balance levels and employee attrition.

---

# Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive filtering to allow users to explore workforce trends from multiple perspectives.

### Interactive Slicers

- Department
- Age Group
- Gender
- Business Travel

### Interactive Features

- Cross-filtering between visuals
- Interactive workforce exploration
- Dynamic filtering across dashboard pages

---

# Dashboard Design

The dashboard was designed using a clean two-page layout with a professional blue color theme.

Design considerations include:

- Consistent color palette
- Executive-friendly layout
- Minimal visual clutter
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Business-focused KPIs
- Interactive filtering
- Consistent visual formatting across both pages

---

# Key Business Insights

The dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify:

- Overall employee attrition rate
- Departments with the highest employee turnover
- Job roles experiencing the greatest attrition
- Workforce demographic distribution
- Impact of overtime on employee attrition
- Relationship between business travel and attrition
- Income differences between retained and departed employees
- Attrition trends by employee tenure
- Employee satisfaction and work-life balance patterns
