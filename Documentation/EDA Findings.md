# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Findings

## Overview

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) was conducted to understand the structure, composition, and characteristics of the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset before performing business analysis.

The analysis focused on employee demographics, organizational structure, compensation, experience, and employee satisfaction metrics.

---

## Employee Overview

- The dataset contains **1,470 employee records**.
- Employees are distributed across **3 departments**.
- The organization consists of **9 unique job roles**.
- Employees represent **6 different education fields**.

---

## Employee Demographics

- The workforce consists of **882 male employees (60%)** and **588 female employees (40%)**.
- Employee ages range from **18 to 60 years**, with an average age of **36.92 years**.
- Most employees are **married (673)**, followed by **single (470)** and **divorced (327)**.
- The most common education level is **Bachelor's degree (572 employees)**, followed by **Master's degree (398 employees)**.

---

## Organizational Structure

- **Research & Development** is the largest department with **961 employees**.
- **Sales** employs **446 employees**.
- **Human Resources** is the smallest department with **63 employees**.

Among job roles:

- **Sales Executive** is the most common role (**326 employees**).
- **Research Scientist** (**292 employees**) and **Laboratory Technician** (**259 employees**) are also major workforce groups.
- **Human Resources** is the smallest job role with **52 employees**.

---

## Business Travel and Overtime

- Most employees travel **rarely (1,043 employees)**.
- **277 employees** travel frequently.
- **150 employees** do not travel for business.

Regarding overtime:

- **1,054 employees** do not work overtime.
- **416 employees** work overtime.

---

## Compensation

Employee compensation shows considerable variation across the organization.

Key statistics include:

- Average monthly income: **$6,502.93**
- Monthly income ranges from **$1,009** to **$19,999**
- Average daily rate: **$802.49**
- Average hourly rate: **$65.89**

These figures indicate a workforce consisting of multiple job levels and salary bands.

---

## Employee Experience

Employee experience varies considerably across the workforce.

Key observations include:

- Average total working experience: **11.28 years**
- Average tenure at the company: **7.01 years**
- Total working experience ranges from **0 to 40 years**.
- Years at the company range from **0 to 40 years**.

These findings suggest that the organization employs both newly hired employees and highly experienced professionals.

---

## Employee Satisfaction

The average employee satisfaction metrics are relatively consistent across all measured categories.

| Metric | Average Score |
|---------|--------------:|
| Job Satisfaction | 2.73 |
| Environment Satisfaction | 2.72 |
| Work-Life Balance | 2.76 |
| Job Involvement | 2.73 |
| Relationship Satisfaction | 2.71 |

Overall, the average satisfaction scores are close to **2.7 out of 4**, indicating moderate levels of employee satisfaction throughout the organization.

---

## Summary

The exploratory analysis provided a comprehensive understanding of the workforce before performing business analysis.

The dataset represents a diverse workforce across multiple departments, job roles, compensation levels, and experience ranges. While workforce demographics and organizational structure appear balanced, further business analysis is required to determine which employee characteristics are most strongly associated with attrition.
