# Project Overview

## Introduction

Employee attrition is a significant challenge for organizations, as high employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and impact overall business performance. Understanding the factors that contribute to employee attrition enables organizations to make informed decisions that improve employee retention and workforce planning.

This project analyzes the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset using PostgreSQL and Power BI to identify patterns associated with employee attrition. The project follows a structured data analytics workflow, beginning with data validation and exploratory analysis, followed by business analysis and interactive dashboard development.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations need to understand why employees leave the company and identify the factors that contribute to employee attrition.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- What is the overall employee attrition rate?
- Which departments experience the highest attrition?
- Which job roles have the highest employee turnover?
- Does overtime influence employee attrition?
- Does business travel impact attrition?
- Which employee age groups have the highest attrition?
- How does monthly income relate to employee attrition?
- Does employee satisfaction affect attrition?
- Does work-life balance influence employee retention?
- How does employee tenure relate to attrition?

The answers to these questions provide valuable insights that can support HR decision-making and employee retention strategies.

## Dataset Information

The analysis uses the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Dataset | IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance |
| Format | CSV |
| Total Records | 1,470 |
| Total Columns | 35 |
| Primary Key | EmployeeNumber |

The dataset contains employee demographic information, job-related attributes, compensation details, performance metrics, satisfaction scores, and attrition status.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Validate the quality and integrity of the dataset.
- Explore workforce demographics and employee characteristics.
- Analyze factors associated with employee attrition.
- Identify workforce trends using SQL.
- Generate business insights to support HR decision-making.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for data visualization.
