# Business Insights

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to answer key business questions related to employee attrition, workforce composition, compensation, employee satisfaction, and workplace factors. The insights presented below are derived from SQL analysis of the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset and provide recommendations that can support employee retention, workforce planning, and strategic HR decision-making.

---

## 1. Overall Employee Attrition

### Business Question

What is the overall employee attrition rate?

### Metric

| Metric | Value |
|---------|------:|
| Overall Attrition Rate | 16.12% |
| Employees Left | 237 |
| Employees Retained | 1,233 |

### Key Findings

- The overall employee attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- A total of **237 employees** have left the organization, while **1,233 employees** remain.
- Approximately one out of every six employees has left the company.

### Business Impact

Employee attrition increases recruitment costs, training expenses, and productivity loss. Monitoring turnover is essential for maintaining workforce stability.

### Recommendation

- Monitor attrition trends regularly.
- Conduct exit interviews to identify common reasons for employee departures.
- Develop proactive employee retention strategies.

## 2. Department Attrition

### Business Question

Which departments have the highest employee attrition?

### Department Attrition Rates

| Department | Attrition Rate |
|------------|---------------:|
| Sales | 20.63% |
| Human Resources | 19.05% |
| Research & Development | 13.84% |

### Key Findings

- The **Sales** department experiences the highest attrition rate.
- **Human Resources** also experiences above-average employee turnover.
- **Research & Development** has the lowest attrition rate among the three departments.

### Business Impact

Departments with higher attrition may experience increased hiring costs, knowledge loss, and reduced operational efficiency.

### Recommendation

- Investigate department-specific turnover causes.
- Improve employee engagement within high-risk departments.
- Review leadership and workload distribution.

## 3. Job Role Attrition

### Business Question

Which job roles have the highest employee attrition?

### Highest Attrition Rates

| Job Role | Attrition Rate |
|----------|---------------:|
| Sales Representative | 39.76% |
| Laboratory Technician | 23.94% |
| Human Resources | 23.08% |
| Sales Executive | 17.48% |
| Research Scientist | 16.10% |

### Key Findings

- **Sales Representatives** experience the highest employee turnover.
- Customer-facing and operational roles generally exhibit higher attrition than management positions.

### Business Impact

High turnover in specific job roles can disrupt daily operations and increase recruitment costs.

### Recommendation

- Prioritize retention initiatives for high-turnover job roles.
- Review career development opportunities and workload expectations.

## 4. Overtime and Attrition

### Business Question

Does overtime influence employee attrition?

### Overtime Attrition Rates

| Overtime | Attrition Rate |
|----------|---------------:|
| Yes | 30.53% |
| No | 10.44% |

### Key Findings

- Employees working overtime leave at nearly three times the rate of employees who do not.
- Overtime is one of the strongest indicators associated with employee attrition.

### Business Impact

Excessive overtime may contribute to employee burnout, reduced job satisfaction, and increased turnover.

### Recommendation

- Monitor overtime workloads.
- Balance staffing levels to reduce excessive overtime.
- Encourage healthy work-life balance practices.

---

## 5. Business Travel

### Business Question

Does business travel affect employee attrition?

### Attrition by Business Travel

| Business Travel | Attrition Rate |
|----------------|---------------:|
| Travel Frequently | 24.91% |
| Travel Rarely | 14.96% |
| Non-Travel | 8.00% |

### Key Findings

- Employees who travel frequently have the highest attrition.
- Attrition decreases as travel requirements become less frequent.

### Business Impact

Frequent travel may contribute to employee fatigue and reduced work-life balance.

### Recommendation

- Review travel requirements.
- Consider virtual meetings where appropriate.
- Provide additional support for employees with extensive travel schedules.
